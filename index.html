Larder, a high-conversion theme for home and kitchen stores

Larder is an Online Store 2.0 Shopify theme designed for merchants who sell housewares, kitchen tools, table goods, and pantry products. It keeps the path from first look to checkout short, with a sticky add to cart button, trust badges, and a shelf-style product layout.

Install Larder

  1. In Shopify admin, go to Online Store, then Themes.
  2. If you bought Larder from the Shopify Theme Store, add it from the Theme Store. If you have a zip file, click Add theme, then Upload zip file, and choose the Larder theme zip.
  3. Click Publish when you are ready to make it the live theme, or open Customize to set it up first.

After install, add your logo and favicon, choose a palette, and assign a featured collection on the home page.

Palettes and typography

Open Theme settings to change the look:

  • Palette: Larder (warm parchment and copper, uses the color pickers), Porcelain (chalk and sage), or Night pantry (dark shelves and brass).
  • Fonts: Outfit for body text and Fraunces for headings by default. Both are Shopify font picker fonts, with bold and italic variants loaded automatically.
  • Color pickers apply when the Larder palette is selected. Each background color has a matching text color so you can keep contrast readable.

Product page

The product page includes the product title, vendor, price, compare-at price, unit price, description, option swatches, quantity, add to cart, accelerated checkout, Shop Pay installments, pickup availability, gift card recipient fields, and selling plans when you use subscriptions. Variant images update when a customer selects an option.

Collection and search

Collection and search pages support filtering and sorting, including availability, price, type, vendor, and variant options when filters are set up in your admin. Empty collections show a clear message. Product cards can show a sale badge when a compare-at price is set.

Cart

The cart page shows line item titles, images, option values, quantities, unit prices, discounts, selling plans, an order note, taxes-included text when needed, checkout, and accelerated checkout buttons.

FAQ

Can I use Larder with a reviews app?

Yes. Add the app as an app block in the Product reviews section. The placeholder text is only shown when no app block is present.

Does the theme include a reviews or discount app?

No. Larder stays within theme features and does not depend on a paid app.

How do I change the home page shelf?

In the theme editor, open the Featured collection section, choose a collection, and set the layout to Shelf, Grid, or Ledger.

Where do country and language selectors appear?

They appear automatically when you sell in multiple countries or languages, in the header menu on small screens and in the footer.
