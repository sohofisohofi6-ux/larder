Merchant support

Contact the Crockery theme team

Use this form for theme questions and bug reports. We reply within two business days. Critical bugs are fixed as soon as possible. Customizations, app setup, and extra design work are not included in the theme price.

After you send the form, you will receive an automatic email confirming that we received your request. A reCAPTCHA step is required so that confirmation can be sent.

Use your myshopify.com URL, for example http://www.storename.myshopify.com.

Add a screenshot of the issue when you can. Combined uploads must be 10 MB or smaller.

What to include

  • The store URL and whether you are using a published theme or a copy.
  • The page or template where the issue appears, such as the product page or cart.
  • Screenshots, and any theme editor setting you already tried.
